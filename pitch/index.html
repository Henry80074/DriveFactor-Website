DriveFactor Risk Architecture
How it works

A Multi-Layer Risk Architecture

Every road segment carries a unique risk fingerprint, driven by its physical design, surrounding road context, traffic flow and environmental conditions. DriveFactor models all of these factors simultaneously in one continuously-updated engine.

L1

Road environment & collision history

Structural risk grounded in road geometry, junction design, carriageway layout and over a decade of Stats19 recorded collision data. The baseline fingerprint of every segment.

Infrastructure · Collision data
Road geometry · Features
L2

Temporal & environmental conditions

The same road in November rain at dusk carries a categorically different risk profile to a dry July afternoon. Hour, season, and weather impacts are all modelled.

Hour · Season · Weather
Darkness · Precipitation
L3

Traffic volume & speed behaviour

Measured and modelled flow, speeds and driving behaviour. Congestion, unexpected volume surges and speed deviation all drive anomalous risk.

Traffic Volume · Speed
Flow estimates · Behaviour
